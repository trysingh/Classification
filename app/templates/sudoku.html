Sudoku solver Demo

Watches the OpenJEV classification approach solve a puzzle one cell at a time. Two logically-certain techniques (naked and hidden singles) handle most cells for free; a real System-1 model is only consulted when neither applies, with its actual probability distribution shown.

Why this step

Start a board, then press Next step.

How this works

Every empty cell's legal candidates come from real constraint checking: whatever digit already appears in that cell's row, column or 3x3 box is eliminated first, same as solving by hand.

A naked single is a cell with exactly one legal candidate left. A hidden single is a digit that has only one legal cell left somewhere in a row, column or box, even if that cell still has other candidates too -- both are logically certain, no guessing.

Only when neither applies anywhere on the board does a real System-1 model get asked, with the actual probability distribution shown here. Its pick is checked against the puzzle's true solution: right or wrong, that's shown too, rather than hidden.
